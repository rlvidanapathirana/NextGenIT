What is AI Prompting?

Think of AI like a super smart friend who can help you with anything. A prompt is just what you TYPE to ask it. The better you ask, the better the answer you get!

❌ Bad Prompt — Gets a boring answer
"Write an email."
😕 The AI doesn't know who to write to, what the email is about, or what tone to use. So it gives you something boring and useless.
✅ Good Prompt — Gets an amazing answer!
"You are a professional writer. Write a polite follow-up email to my client who hasn't replied in 3 days. Keep it under 100 words and friendly but professional."
✨ Now the AI knows WHO to be, WHAT to write, HOW long, and WHAT tone — perfect answer every time!
💡

Golden Rule: The more details you give the AI, the better its answer will be. Think of it like telling a very smart friend exactly what you need — don't leave anything out!
