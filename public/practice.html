Challenge 1: Work Meeting Email

This prompt is too vague. What's missing?

❌ Weak Prompt: "Write me an email about the meeting."
🤔

Think about it: Who gets this email? When is the meeting? What's it about? What tone? What should people prepare?

✅ Improved Prompt: "You are a professional business communicator. Write a concise, polite email to my team of 8 software developers reminding them about our sprint review meeting this Friday at 2PM (Sri Lanka time). The tone should be friendly but professional. Include: (1) the meeting purpose (review Q3 sprint deliverables), (2) what they should prepare (updated task status in Jira), and (3) a Zoom link placeholder [INSERT ZOOM LINK]. Keep it under 150 words." 💡 What improved: Persona ✅ | Who receives it ✅ | When & what ✅ | What to prepare ✅ | Tone ✅ | Word limit ✅

Challenge 2: Social Media Posts

This is way too vague. What's the AI supposed to do?!

❌ Weak Prompt: "Write social media posts."
🤔

Think about it: Which platform (Instagram/Facebook/LinkedIn)? What business? Who is the audience? What language? How many posts? What tone?

✅ Improved Prompt: "You are a social media manager for a Sri Lankan tech startup. Write 3 engaging Instagram captions promoting our new AI productivity app 'FlowAI'. Target audience: young professionals aged 22–35 in Colombo. Tone: energetic, modern, with a hint of Sri Lankan flavor. Each caption must: (1) be under 100 words, (2) include 1 clear call-to-action ('Download now', 'Try for free'), and (3) end with 3–5 relevant hashtags including #SriLankaTech. Write in English." 💡 What improved: Persona ✅ | Platform ✅ | Product ✅ | Audience ✅ | Tone ✅ | Format ✅ | Number ✅ | Language ✅

Challenge 3: Data Analysis

Generic and useless. What's the actual goal here?

❌ Weak Prompt: "Analyze this data."
🤔

Think about it: What type of data? What analysis is needed? What output format? Who will read the results? What decisions will this inform?

✅ Improved Prompt: "You are a senior business analyst. I will provide monthly sales data for a Sri Lankan retail business (Q1–Q3 2024). Please: (1) Identify the top 3 performing months and briefly explain WHY they may have performed well, (2) Highlight any concerning downward trends with specific numbers, (3) Give 3 specific, actionable recommendations for Q4 to improve sales. Present your findings as a structured report with clear headings and bullet points. Use simple, non-technical language — this report is for the business owner, not a data scientist. [Paste data below]" 💡 What improved: Persona ✅ | What data ✅ | What to find ✅ | Format ✅ | Audience ✅ | Language style ✅

Challenge 4: Sinhala Business Letter

In Sinhala, context is everything. This tells the AI nothing!

❌ Weak Prompt: "ලිපියක් ලියන්න."
🤔

Think about it: Who is writing the letter? To whom? What is the purpose? What tone? How long? What language?

✅ Improved Prompt: "ඔබ professional ව්‍යාපාරික ලේඛකයෙකි. [Company Name] සමාගමේ [Manager Name] වෙත ස්තූතිය පිළිබඳ ගෞරවනීය සිංහල ව්‍යාපාරික ලිපියක් ලියන්න. ලිපිය: (1) ඉතා ගෞරවාදරයෙන් ආරම්භ කරන්න, (2) [Specific Achievement/Project] සඳහා කෘතඥතාව ප්‍රකාශ කරන්න, (3) අනාගතයේ සහයෝගය ලබා ගැනීමට ඉල්ලීමෙන් අවසන් කරන්න. ලිපිය words 150-200 ඇතුළත රකින්න. Formal Sinhala business letter format use කරන්න." 💡 What improved: Persona ✅ | Who writes it ✅ | To whom ✅ | Purpose ✅ | Structure ✅ | Length ✅ | Format ✅

Challenge 5: Student Study Help

A common student mistake — too vague and no format!

❌ Weak Prompt: "Explain photosynthesis."
🤔

Think about it: What level? How long? With examples? What format? Should it include diagrams/steps? For an exam or just to understand?

✅ Improved Prompt: "You are a friendly biology teacher explaining to a 12-year-old Grade 8 student. Explain photosynthesis in the simplest possible way using: (1) a real-life analogy (like a factory or kitchen), (2) the main formula in simple words (not just symbols), and (3) a numbered step-by-step process with maximum 4 steps. No complicated science jargon. Make it interesting and fun! Keep the total explanation under 200 words." 💡 What improved: Persona (teacher) ✅ | Who is the audience (12yr old) ✅ | Format (analogy + steps) ✅ | Tone (fun) ✅ | Word limit ✅ | No jargon ✅

Test What You Learned!

5 questions about AI prompting. Click an answer — instant feedback! Retry as many times as you want.

Question 1: In the T.C.R.E.I framework, what does the letter "C" stand for?
✅ Correct! "C" = Context — the background information about your situation, audience, and constraints. This helps AI understand your world!
❌ Not quite! "C" stands for Context — giving the AI background information like who the audience is, what the situation is, and what constraints you have.
Question 2: You show the AI 4 examples before asking your question. What type of prompting is this called?
✅ Correct! Few-Shot = 3 or more examples. The AI learns your style from those examples and produces consistent, on-brand results!
❌ Not quite! 4 examples = Few-Shot Prompting. Zero-Shot = no examples. Single-Shot = exactly 1 example. Few-Shot = 3 or more!
Question 3: Your AI answer is too generic and has no personality. The FASTEST fix is to add what to your prompt?
✅ Correct! Adding a Persona instantly gives AI a specific voice, expertise, and tone to write from. "You are a friendly doctor" vs "You are a corporate lawyer" gives totally different results!
❌ The best fix is adding a Persona (B)! Saying "You are an expert [X]" gives AI a specific voice and expertise level to write from — completely transforms the answer!
Question 4: Which AI platform is BEST for research with real, clickable source links?
✅ Correct! Perplexity AI is specifically designed for research — every single answer comes with clickable links to the real sources it used!
❌ Perplexity AI (C) is the research specialist! It always shows you clickable source links for every answer, so you can verify everything it tells you.
Question 5: What is the best thing to do if your AI answer is NOT what you wanted?
✅ Correct! Iterate = keep improving your prompt. Add missing context, change the format, add a persona, or break it into smaller steps. The best prompts are written through iteration!
❌ The answer is Iterate (D)! Fix your prompt by adding context, changing wording, adding a persona, or breaking it into steps. Never give up after one try — all good prompters iterate!

📌 Quick Reference

Prompting Cheat Sheet

Save this or screenshot it — your quick reference for writing great prompts every time!

⚡ The Perfect Prompt Formula

T

Task — What should AI DO?

"Write / List / Explain / Create / Translate / Summarize / Analyze..."

P

Persona — Who should AI BE?

"You are a [expert/teacher/lawyer/chef/marketing manager]..."

C

Context — What's the situation?

"For my [audience]. Budget is [X]. It's for [purpose]. Tone should be [X]..."

F

Format — How should the answer look?

"In a numbered list / as a table / in bullet points / as a formal email / under [X] words..."

L

Language — Sinhala or English?

"Write the response in Sinhala / in formal English / in simple everyday language..."

💡

Example putting it all together: "You are a friendly biology teacher (Persona). Explain photosynthesis (Task) to a 12-year-old Grade 8 student (Context). Use a simple analogy and numbered steps (Format). Keep it under 200 words, no jargon (Constraints). Write in simple English (Language)."
