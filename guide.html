The Prompting Framework

Learn the 5 magic ingredients that turn average prompts into amazing ones. Simple enough for a 10-year-old to understand — and powerful enough for professionals!

🤔 Step 1 — Understand the Basics

What Exactly is a Prompt?

A prompt is just the message you type to an AI. Like texting a smart friend — but this friend knows EVERYTHING and can do almost ANYTHING... if you ask correctly!

1

You Type Something (the Prompt)

Example: "Write a birthday message for my 10-year-old sister who loves cats 🐱"

2

AI Reads Your Prompt

The AI looks for clues — who is it for? what tone? how long? what topic? The more clues you give, the better!

3

AI Gives You an Answer

More details you gave = better the answer! Missing details = boring, generic answer 😐

🎯 The Magic Formula

The T.C.R.E.I Framework

5 magic ingredients that make every prompt powerful. You don't need ALL 5 every time — but using more = much better results!

T
Task
කාර්යය / பணி
Tell AI what to do. Use action words like "Write", "List", "Explain", "Create", "Translate".
✅ "Write 5 ideas..."
✅ "Explain in simple words..."
❌ "I need something..."
C
Context
සන්දර්භය / சூழல்
Give background info — who is it for? what is the situation? what rules apply?
✅ "...for my 10-year-old who loves cats"
✅ "budget is Rs.5,000"
❌ (no context = generic!)
R
References
යොමු / குறிப்புகள்
Show AI examples of what you want. Like showing a chef a photo of the dish you want!
✅ "Write like this example: [example]"
✅ "Use the same tone as..."
E
Evaluate
ඇගයීම / மதிப்பீடு
Check the answer you got. Is it correct? Is anything missing? Is the tone right?
🔍 Is it accurate?
🔍 Is the format right?
🔍 Would a real person use this?
I
Iterate
නැවත ලිවීම / மீண்டும்
Improve your prompt based on what you got. Best prompts get better over time!
✏️ "Make it shorter..."
✏️ "Add more detail about..."
✏️ "Change the tone to..."
🎓

Real Example: "You are an expert chef (Persona). Write a 3-ingredient chocolate cake recipe (Task) for my 8-year-old nephew who is learning to bake (Context). Use simple words and number each step (Format). Keep it under 200 words (Constraint)." — This prompt will get you an AMAZING answer! 🍰

🎭 Two Secret Weapons

Persona & Format

Two extra tricks that make a HUGE difference in your answers!

🎭

Persona — "You are a..."

Tell AI WHO to be! A doctor answers differently than a teacher — even about the same topic!

"You are a 5-star hotel manager. Write a complaint reply..." → Professional, polite tone
"You are a friendly teacher. Explain photosynthesis..." → Simple, fun tone
"You are a Sri Lankan tax consultant. Explain income tax..." → Local, relevant!
📋

Format — "Show it as a..."

Tell AI HOW to present the answer! This saves you tons of editing time.

"...in a numbered list" → 1. Step one 2. Step two...
"...as a comparison table" → Side-by-side comparison!
"...in bullet points, under 100 words" → Short and organized!
"...as a formal email" → Correct email structure!
👀 See the Difference

Good Prompts vs Bad Prompts

Compare these side by side and spot what makes the good ones WORK!

❌ Too Vague
"Give me ideas for a gift."
😕 Who is it for? Budget? What do they like? AI has NO idea — so it gives boring, generic ideas.
✅ Much Better!
"Give me 5 birthday gift ideas for a 29-year-old who loves skiing. My budget is Rs. 5,000. List as bullet points with price estimate."
✨ Number ✅ Who ✅ Budget ✅ Format ✅ = Perfect answer!
❌ Sinhala - Too Short
"ලිපියක් ලියන්න."
😕 AI doesn't know: Who? To whom? What topic? How long? Total failure!
✅ Sinhala - Full Prompt
"ඔබ professional ව්‍යාපාරික ලේඛකයෙකි. [Company] ට සිංහල ව්‍යාපාරික ලිපියක් ලියන්න. Tone: ගෞරවනීය. Length: 200 words ඇතුළත."
✨ Persona ✅ Language ✅ Tone ✅ Length ✅ = Perfect Sinhala letter!
📸 Reference Techniques

Shot Types — How Many Examples?

Including examples in your prompt is like showing AI a sample of what you want. More examples = more consistent results!

0

Zero-Shot

No examples. AI uses its own knowledge. Works for simple, clear tasks.

"Translate to Sinhala: 'The meeting starts at 2PM'"
🟡 Best for: simple tasks
1

Single-Shot

Give ONE example. AI learns the style and tone from it.

"Example: 'Bold taste every day.' Now write one for our new headphones."
🟢 Best for: style matching
3+

Few-Shot

Give 3+ examples. AI spots the pattern and produces highly consistent results!

"Here are 3 examples: [1][2][3]. Now write 5 more in the same style."
🟢 Best for: consistent brand content
🔄 Iterate — Fix Bad Answers

AI Gave a Bad Answer? Try These!

Don't give up! Even experts fix and improve their prompts. Here are 4 proven ways to get a better answer:

🔍

1. Add a Missing Ingredient

Check T.C.R.E.I. Did you forget audience? Format? Length? Add the missing piece!

"Make it shorter" / "Add formal tone" / "Include a table"
✂️

2. Break Into Small Steps

Split your big request into 2-3 smaller, clearer steps!

Step 1: "List 3 main ideas"
Step 2: "Write about idea #1"
🔁

3. Say It Differently

Try different words for the same request. Small changes make big differences!

"Write a marketing plan" → "Tell a story about how a customer's life improved..."
🎯

4. Add Constraints

Limit the answer! Saying "under 100 words" or "simple language" actually makes answers BETTER!

"Under 150 words" / "Explain like I'm 10" / "In Sinhala"
