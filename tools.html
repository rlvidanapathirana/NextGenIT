All 7 AI Tools Explained

Everything you need to know about each AI tool — simple, honest, and straight to the point!

🗣️
Dialog AI
dialog.lk/ai
🌐 Best Sinhala AI Free Available

Sri Lanka's FIRST local AI! Built specifically to understand and write in Sinhala. Perfect for Sri Lankan professionals who need to write formal documents, emails, and reports in Sinhala.

🇱🇰 Best Sinhala AI in Sri Lanka! Made specifically for Sinhala writing — formal letters, business emails, office documents. Dialog users may have special access!
Sinhala formal letters & emails
Business documents in Sinhala
Sinhala to English translation
Official correspondence in Sinhala
🤖
ChatGPT
chatgpt.com
🏆 Most Popular Free Available Sinhala OK

The world's most popular AI! An all-rounder that's amazing at writing, coding, analysis, creative work, and explaining anything. Understands Sinhala and can reply in Sinhala. Has thousands of specialized "GPT" add-ons!

Writing emails, reports, proposals
Coding and debugging
Creative writing and storytelling
Explaining complex topics simply
HR documents and policies
✨
Google Gemini
gemini.google.com
🌟 Great Sinhala Free Available Google Integration

Google's AI that works INSIDE Gmail, Docs, Sheets, and Slides. Gemini responds VERY WELL in Sinhala — one of the best for Sinhala content! Can also read images and analyze charts.

🌟 Gemini is excellent in Sinhala! It understands Sinhala well and gives high-quality Sinhala responses — formal writing, explanations, stories, and more. Great choice for Sinhala content!
Sinhala writing & explanations
Gmail, Google Docs & Sheets tasks
Image analysis and description
YouTube video scripts
Google Slides presentations
⚡
Grok (xAI)
grok.com
🎯 Human-like Style Free Available Real-time News

Elon Musk's AI from xAI. Grok replies in a MORE HUMAN, casual, and personable style than other AIs — it doesn't sound robotic! Has real-time internet access and can create short videos from photos. Great for social media content and news.

Social media content (feels real!)
Current news & trending topics
Human-sounding copywriting
Casual explanations & debates
LinkedIn & Twitter/X posts
🧠
Claude AI
claude.ai
📄 Long Documents Free Available Deep Analysis

Made by Anthropic. Claude is exceptional at reading very LONG documents — up to 200,000 words in one go! Perfect for analyzing reports, contracts, research papers. Also great for thoughtful, nuanced writing and careful analysis.

Analyzing 100+ page documents
Contract and legal document review
Research paper summaries
Deep business strategy analysis
Careful, nuanced writing & editing
🔍
Perplexity AI
perplexity.ai
🔗 Cites Sources Free Available

The BEST AI for research! Every answer comes with links to the real websites it got the information from — so you can verify everything! Great for finding current news, fact-checking, and research projects.

Market research with real sources
Fact-checking claims
Competitor analysis
Current news summaries
Sri Lanka regulation research
🔷
Microsoft Copilot
copilot.microsoft.com
💼 Microsoft Office Free Available

AI built directly INSIDE Microsoft Office! Use it in Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Outlook, and Teams without switching tabs. Perfect for workplaces that use Microsoft 365 every day.

Create PowerPoint slides instantly
Excel formulas & data analysis
Draft emails in Outlook
Write Word documents & reports
Summarize Teams meetings
